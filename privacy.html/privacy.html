Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Odyssey Lands project handles information when you use our website, Discord community, or the Tidewarden Discord bot (together, the "Services").

1. Information We Handle

  • Discord: When you use the bot or community, Discord may share basic account information (such as your username, ID, and avatar) with the Services. Tidewarden stores only what it needs to function, such as your user ID for command use.
  • Website: The Odyssey Lands website is a static page with no tracking scripts, no cookies for analytics, and no account system. We process no personal data on the website itself.
  • Minecraft: The community server may log usernames and chat messages for operational and moderation purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate and improve the Services: responding to bot commands, moderating the community, keeping the Minecraft server safe, and answering support questions.

3. What We Do Not Do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not use your data for purposes other than those described here.

4. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed. You can ask us to remove data we hold about you at any time by contacting us through the community.

5. Data Shared With Third Parties

The Services rely on providers such as Discord, GitHub Pages, and Aternos. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not share your information with them except as necessary to run the Services.

6. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle, but no method of transmission over the internet is fully secure. You use the Services at your own risk.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be raised in our Discord community.
